# Noma (restaurant)

The Copenhagen restaurant that made foraging the language of fine dining, was voted the world's best five times, then closed its own dining room because the economics never worked.

**Location:** 55.6829, 12.6105

**Categories:** Christianshavn, Companies based in Copenhagen Municipality, Michelin-starred restaurants in Denmark, Restaurants established in 2003, Restaurants in Copenhagen

## Introduction

"It's unsustainable. Financially and emotionally, as an employer and as a human being, it just doesn't work." That was Rene Redzepi in January 2023, explaining why the restaurant voted best in the world five separate times would stop serving dinner. Noma had spent nearly two decades persuading the rest of the profession that Nordic weeds, seaweed and fermented everything could carry a three-star tasting menu. It had succeeded so completely that chefs from Lima to Melbourne were foraging their own back yards. And it still could not make the numbers add up - not once it started paying everyone in its kitchen.

## A Warehouse on the Greenland Quay

The first Noma opened in 2003 at Strandgade 93, in an old harbourside warehouse in Christianshavn. The building stands on the Greenlandic Trading Square, which for two centuries handled the traffic between Denmark and its North Atlantic possessions - dried fish, salted herring, whale oil and skins came off ships here and were stored before being sold on into Europe. In 2003 the warehouse was converted into North Atlantic House, a cultural centre for that same region, and Redzepi opened his restaurant in it with the entrepreneur Claus Meyer. The name is a compression of two Danish words, nordisk and mad: Nordic food. Space Copenhagen designed the interior. Redzepi had trained at The French Laundry, El Bulli, Kong Hans Kaelder and Le Jardin des Sens, and what he brought back was less a technique than a question - what if the pantry stopped at the coastline?

## Rearranging the Map

In 2010 Noma took the top spot on Restaurant magazine's World's 50 Best list, unseating El Bulli after four consecutive years. It won again in 2011, and in 2012, when the magazine called it "the standard bearer for the New Nordic movement." It lost to El Celler de Can Roca in 2013, took the title back in 2014, and won a fifth time in 2021. Michelin was slower: two stars from 2008 to 2020, three from 2021. The cooking was an argument rather than a cuisine - Meyer described it as an interpretation of Nordic food rather than the thing itself. Diners assembled "The Hen and the Egg" themselves at the table from potato chips, a wild duck egg, slightly damp hay, herbs, hay oil, thyme, butter and wild garlic sauce.

## Away Games

Noma travelled, and the travelling became part of the method. In summer 2012 the whole operation decamped to Claridge's in Mayfair for ten days, serving a nine-course menu at 195 pounds a head that included scones and clotted cream, a Lancashire hotpot made with British ingredients, and live ants foraged in Denmark and flown to London. Japan followed in 2015, Australia in 2016. Noma Mexico opened in Tulum in April 2017, built on a partnership with Traspatio Maya, a nonprofit network of fifteen Maya communities supplying white Naal Teel corn, Ixil onions, Xtop pepita, bee larvae and Melipona honey from the Calakmul Biosphere Reserve. Back home, the restaurant reopened on 15 February 2018 at Refshalevej 96 after a year's hiatus, leaving Strandgade to Restaurant Barr. When the pandemic closed the dining room in May 2020, Noma reopened as a wine and burger bar.

## The Arithmetic of the Kitchen

In 2022 the Financial Times reported what the industry had long known and rarely said aloud: roughly thirty of the people cooking at Noma were unpaid interns, working alongside thirty-four paid chefs, some of them putting in up to seventy hours a week. A Vice investigation the following year found about half of the sixty-person kitchen still unpaid. These were not observers. They were staff, doing the prep and the plating that a three-star menu requires, and absorbing the cost of Copenhagen rent to do it. In October 2022 Noma began paying all of them, which added an estimated fifty to seventy thousand dollars a month to its wage bill. Three months later, Redzepi announced the restaurant would end regular service. He named that cost as one of the reasons.

## What Is Left

The last regular dinners were served not in Copenhagen but in Kyoto, where Noma ran a pop-up from 8 October to 18 December 2024. Since then it has operated as a food innovation lab, developing products and techniques, with occasional residencies rather than a dining room - a residency in Los Angeles was announced in July 2025 for a tentative start in spring 2026. Then, in March 2026, the New York Times reported that Redzepi had physically abused employees over a period of years; thirty former staff described being hit by him as routine. Redzepi responded that while he did not recognise every detail, "I can see enough of my past behaviour reflected in them to understand that my actions were harmful to people who worked with me," and apologised to "those who have suffered under my leadership, my bad judgment, or my anger." He resigned later that month, saying an apology was not enough and that he took responsibility for his own actions. Noma changed what restaurants cook. The reckoning over what that cost the people cooking it arrived considerably later.

## Flight Context

Noma's Refshalevej site sits at 55.6829 N, 12.6105 E on Refshaleoen, the former shipyard island on the eastern side of Copenhagen's harbour. From 1,500-3,000 ft AGL the island is easy to pick out: a flat industrial spit north of Christianshavn, marked by the ski slope roof of the Amager Bakke waste-to-energy plant just to its south and by the wind turbines along the harbour mouth. The original Strandgade premises lie about 2 km southwest across the Inner Harbour. Copenhagen Kastrup (EKCH) is roughly 6 km south-southeast, with approach paths crossing the water nearby; Roskilde (EKRK) is about 33 km west. Sea fog off the Oresund is common in spring and autumn.
