# St. Jude Children's Research Hospital

A struggling comedian's seven-dollar prayer became St. Jude Children's Research Hospital in Memphis, a research center for children's catastrophic diseases that does not charge its patients for care.

**Location:** 35.1529, -90.0431

**Categories:** Children's hospitals in the United States, Hospitals in Memphis, Tennessee, Medical research institutes in the United States, 1962 establishments in Tennessee, Philanthropy

## Introduction

Danny Thomas was a comedian who could not catch a break, living paycheck to paycheck, when his first child was about to be born. At Mass in Detroit he emptied his wallet into the offering box. It held $7. He prayed for the intercession of Saint Jude Thaddeus, asking for a way to provide for his family, and about a week later he landed a gig that paid ten times what he had given. Thomas promised the saint that if his career succeeded, he would build him a shrine. The shrine he eventually built stands in Memphis, and it is a hospital for children with catastrophic diseases.

## No Child Should Die in the Dawn of Life

Success did come. Thomas starred in Make Room for Daddy, later known as The Danny Thomas Show, and on February 4, 1962, before a crowd of 9,000 people, he founded St. Jude Children's Research Hospital. He had help from Lemuel Diggs and from his close friend Anthony Abraham, a Miami automobile dealer. The hospital was built on a single premise: "no child should die in the dawn of life."

Thomas was a Maronite Catholic, and the hospital carries the name of his patron saint, but St. Jude is a secular institution, not affiliated with any religious organization. Its first director, Donald Pinkel, served from 1962 to 1973. Its focus is the catastrophic diseases of childhood, particularly leukemia and other cancers. Patients are not charged for care, and St. Jude treats young people up to age 21, and for some conditions up to 25.

## A Yacht, a Game Piece and a Rocket

Keeping that promise takes money, more than $2 billion a year to sustain and grow the hospital by its own account, and St. Jude's fundraising history is full of surprises. In January 1964, Elvis Presley bought the former presidential yacht USS Potomac for $55,000 and gave it to the hospital to sell. In 1995 an anonymous letter postmarked in Dallas arrived containing a winning $1 million McDonald's Monopoly game piece. Game rules barred transferring prizes, and the piece turned out to have been sent by someone involved in an embezzlement scheme against McDonald's, yet the company waived the rule and paid the $50,000 annual installments anyway.

The gifts have kept coming in new forms. Danny's daughter, Marlo Thomas, grew up supporting the mission and became its national outreach director in 1991. Chili's pledged $50 million in 2007 toward the seven-story Chili's Care Center. In 2021 the SpaceX Inspiration4 mission raised over $243 million for St. Jude, and in 2025 the YouTuber Ryan Trahan raised over $11 million with a 50-states road-trip fundraiser.

## Science Beside the Bedside

The "research" in the name is meant literally. Peter C. Doherty of St. Jude shared the 1996 Nobel Prize in Physiology or Medicine for work on how the immune system kills virus-infected cells. In 2010 the hospital launched the Pediatric Cancer Genome Project with Washington University, seeking the mutations that drive cancer in hopes of finding new targets for treatment. The Marlo Thomas Center for Global Education and Collaboration opened in 2014, and in 2017 the St. Jude Graduate School of Biomedical Sciences welcomed its first class of PhD students.

The mission reaches well past Memphis. An International Outreach Program works to improve survival for children with catastrophic illnesses worldwide, and an affiliate, the Children's Cancer Center of Lebanon, opened in Beirut in 2002. Closer to home, St. Jude works with Le Bonheur Children's Medical Center, where its patients may undergo procedures such as brain surgery. In September 2026, U.S. News & World Report ranked St. Jude third among the country's pediatric cancer hospitals, after placing it in the top 10 for 18 consecutive years through 2025.

## The Weight of the Promise

In fiscal 2021 St. Jude received $2 billion in donations, and donations make up about 74 percent of its total budget. St. Jude says that about 89 percent of the funds it needs to sustain and grow in the years ahead must be raised by its fundraising charity, ALSAC. Families are not billed for treatment, though St. Jude covers some, not all, cancer-related costs. Charity Navigator gives its fundraising charity, ALSAC-St. Jude, a four-star rating for accountability and finance, according to the hospital's own site.

None of that scale was part of the original bargain. What Thomas promised was a shrine, in exchange for a break he was not sure would ever come. What stands in Memphis more than six decades later is a place where children are treated without being charged for their care.

## Flight Context

St. Jude's campus lies at 35.153°N, 90.043°W, just north of downtown Memphis. From 2,000-3,000 ft AGL, look north of the downtown towers for a cluster of large modern hospital and research buildings. Memphis sits under Class B airspace, so coordinate with approach control. General DeWitt Spain Airport (FAA identifier M01; no ICAO code) is about 3 nm north. The main Memphis airport, Frederick W. Smith International Airport (KMEM), is about 7 nm south-southeast, and West Memphis Municipal Airport (KAWM) is about 9.5 nm west across the river in Arkansas. Watch for medical helicopter traffic around the downtown hospitals; summer brings haze and thunderstorms.
