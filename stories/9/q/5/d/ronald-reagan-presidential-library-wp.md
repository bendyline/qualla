## Air Force One on the Ground

The library's most spectacular artifact arrived in 2005: SAM 27000, the Boeing VC-137C that served as Air Force One for seven presidencies, from Richard Nixon through Bill Clinton. The aircraft is 152 feet long and required the construction of a dedicated 90,000-square-foot pavilion to house it. ## A Resting Place on a California Hillside
