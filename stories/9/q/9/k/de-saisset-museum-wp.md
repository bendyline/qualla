# de Saisset Museum

A free art and history museum on the campus of Santa Clara University built from a bequest honoring a talented painter who died young, housing California mission artifacts alongside contemporary art.

**Location:** 37.3494, -121.9389

**Categories:** Museums in Santa Clara, California, Art museums in California, Santa Clara University

## Introduction

Ernest de Saisset died at 35, leaving behind a body of paintings that showed genuine talent cut short. His sister Isabel, who had watched her brother study art in San Francisco and Paris only to lose him before his career could fully develop, donated his paintings and a substantial sum to Santa Clara University to build a museum in his memory. The de Saisset Museum, which opened in 1955, has grown far beyond its origins as a memorial gallery. Today it houses a permanent collection spanning California mission artifacts, contemporary art, and the university's own history.

## A Parent's Memorial

The de Saisset family's connection to Santa Clara University ran deep. Ernest had attended the Jesuit institution before pursuing art studies. When his sister Isabel de Saisset decided to honor his memory, she chose the campus as the natural home for both his paintings and the museum that would house them. The original building was modest, but subsequent expansions have transformed it into a significant regional museum with gallery space, climate-controlled storage for its permanent collection, and facilities for traveling exhibitions.

## Mission to Modernity

The museum's permanent collection bridges the gap between Santa Clara's mission heritage and its present as a university in the heart of Silicon Valley. California mission-era artifacts, including items from Mission Santa Clara de Asis, which the university campus surrounds, share space with contemporary art installations and works from the university's growing collection. The museum hosts rotating exhibitions that range from faculty and student art shows to nationally touring exhibitions, giving the campus community and the public access to art that might otherwise require a trip to San Francisco or San Jose's own museums.

## Free and Open

Like many university museums, the de Saisset charges no admission, making it accessible to anyone who walks onto the Santa Clara campus. The museum serves as both an educational resource for the university and a public cultural amenity for the city of Santa Clara and the surrounding communities. Its collection of Ernest de Saisset's paintings remains on display, a permanent reminder of the young artist whose early death prompted his sister to create a gift that has served the public for more than six decades.
