# Universal Studios Florida

Conceived in 1982 to take on Disney and built as both a theme park and a working studio, Universal Studios Florida has spent more than three decades tearing down and rebuilding itself around a central lagoon, from Jaws and Kongfrontation to Diagon Alley and Minion Land.

## Introduction

Universal had the idea first. In 1982 the studio began contemplating a Florida park to compete with Disney, and when it pitched that idea, Michael Eisner was running Paramount. By 1987 Eisner was running Disney, and Disney was building a studio park of its own at Walt Disney World. Universal had gone public with its Orlando plans, but Disney was in a better position to fast-track construction and open sooner. Universal Studios Florida finally opened its gates on June 7, 1990, the first park of what would become the Universal Orlando Resort.

## A Park That Was Also a Studio

The inspiration came from California, where Universal Studios Hollywood had thrived in the 1980s on studio tour attractions like King Kong Encounter. The Florida park, built for an estimated $631 million and owned half by MCA Universal and half by the leisure company The Rank Organisation, was meant to be both an amusement park and an active studio lot. Nickelodeon Studios became one of its pillars, and in the park's first month visitors could glimpse live production on its soundstages. The opening credits of the Nickelodeon shows Roundhouse and All That were filmed around the park's New York area. In late 1990, after a fire swept the Universal backlot in Hollywood on November 6 and destroyed the exterior set, director John Landis moved to Florida for the final six days of filming on the comedy Oscar, the first picture shot at the Orlando studio. Interest in making films in the Orlando area faded quickly, though, and the backlot side of the dream was soon abandoned. The soundstages never went entirely quiet: the 2010–11 season of Family Feud, Steve Harvey's first as host, was taped here, and CNBC's 2018 revival of Deal or No Deal was filmed in Soundstage 21.

## Where Amity Island Used to Be

The park has reinvented itself again and again. Two of the early attractions were developed in close collaboration with Steven Spielberg, and the list of rides that have come and gone reads like a history of the blockbuster: Kongfrontation, Jaws, Back to the Future: The Ride, Twister...Ride It Out, T2-3D: Battle Across Time, Shrek 4-D. The most dramatic transformation replaced Amity Island, a section modeled on the seaside town from Jaws, which was completely demolished to make way for The Wizarding World of Harry Potter – Diagon Alley. It opened on July 8, 2014, with Harry Potter and the Escape from Gringotts and a Hogwarts Express train linking it to Hogsmeade at Universal Islands of Adventure. Amity has not been entirely forgotten. A set of shark jawbones hangs behind the herbs and potions of Mr. Mulpepper's Apothecary, and the old Amity Island restrooms are still open outside Diagon Alley. Only one ride has survived from opening day: E.T. Adventure, the oldest attraction in the park.

## Eight Worlds Around a Lagoon

Today the park arranges eight themed areas around a large lagoon. Going clockwise from the entrance, they are Minion Land on Illumination Ave, which opened in August 2023; New York, where a statue honors longtime MCA/Universal executive Lew Wasserman; San Francisco, home from 2018 until August 2026 to Fast & Furious: Supercharged; London and Diagon Alley; World Expo; Springfield, which counted as part of World Expo until 2016; DreamWorks Land, which opened in June 2024 on the site of Woody Woodpecker's KidZone; and Hollywood, where Schwab's Pharmacy and Mel's Drive-In recreate real Hollywood eateries. About 9.5 million guests came through in 2024, placing it among the most visited theme parks in the world.

## Horror Nights and Macy's Balloons

The calendar keeps the park changing, too. From late September into early November, Halloween Horror Nights turns the streets into a haunted playground of scare zones and haunted houses inspired by horror films, television and original concepts; in 2023 it won Best Halloween Event of 2022 at the Golden Ticket Awards. December brings a holiday parade with authentic balloons from the Macy's Thanksgiving Day Parade, and late winter and spring bring a Mardi Gras parade where employees and invited guests toss beads to the crowds. Graduating classes take over the parks for Grad Bash and Gradventure each spring. Ownership shifted behind the scenes as well. The Rank Group sold its half-stake to Blackstone in 2000, and NBCUniversal bought Blackstone's half in 2011 for about $1 billion, taking full ownership of a resort that now includes Islands of Adventure, Volcano Bay and, since 2025, Epic Universe.

## Flight Context

Universal Studios Florida sits at about 28.475°N, 81.47°W in southwest Orlando. From 2,000–3,000 feet, look for the park's eight themed areas ringing a large central lagoon, with the rest of the Universal Orlando Resort nearby. The airspace is busy: Orlando Executive Airport (KORL) lies about 8.5 nm to the east-northeast and Orlando International (KMCO) about 9 nm to the east-southeast, with Kissimmee Gateway (KISM) about 11 nm to the south. Central Florida summers bring near-daily afternoon thunderstorms, so mornings and the drier winter months offer the clearest views.
