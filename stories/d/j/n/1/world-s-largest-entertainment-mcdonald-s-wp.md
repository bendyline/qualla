# World's Largest Entertainment McDonald's

Orlando's multi-story Epic McD, billed as the largest McDonald's in the world by floor area, has brick oven pizza, Belgian waffles, more than 100 arcade games and a 22-foot PlayPlace.

**Location:** 28.4505, -81.4715

**Categories:** McDonald's buildings and structures, Restaurants established in 1976, Restaurants in Florida, Orlando, Florida

## Introduction

Brick oven pizza. Belgian waffles. Pasta made to order, omelettes, and a "Gourmet Bistro" menu. None of it is what most people expect when they pull into a McDonald's, and that is the point. The World's Largest Entertainment McDonald's in Orlando, Florida, serves more than 50 items on top of the standard menu, dishes that few other McDonald's offer. The restaurant, known today as Epic McD and once called Mickey D's, first opened in 1976, and it has grown into something closer to an indoor amusement center that happens to sell fries.

## Measured in Square Feet

The "world's largest" in the name is a claim about floor space. With a floor area of about 19,000 square feet spread across two or three stories, depending on who is counting, the Orlando restaurant is billed as the largest McDonald's on Earth by square footage, though some accounts call it only the largest in the United States and one ranks it second, behind a McDonald's at Disneyland Paris. Height is another matter. A McDonald's in Kaohsiung, Taiwan, is believed to be taller, so the Orlando branch holds its title strictly on floor space.

The building standing today is not the original. The first structure closed in 2015 and was demolished, and a new one was built next door, reopening in 2016. It keeps the same outsized ambitions, and it is open 24 hours a day.

## Off the Usual Menu

The kitchen here works from a far longer list than a standard McDonald's. Alongside the familiar burgers and fries sit brick oven pizza, Belgian waffles, customized pasta dishes and omelettes, plus more choices on the Gourmet Bistro menu and an expanded dessert menu that includes ice cream. A "Create Your Own" menu lets diners build their own orders.

That makes a stop here different from the usual drive-through routine. Visitors can order the familiar meal, or they can order something that few other McDonald's in the chain will make for them.

## Games, Songs and a 22-Foot Climb

The "entertainment" half of the name is just as literal. The restaurant holds more than 100 arcade games, enough to turn a meal into an afternoon. A Mac Tonight animatronic show is said to perform for diners, and a PlayPlace towers 22 feet, or about 6.7 meters, high, a feature the restaurant has offered since 1996.

The welcome starts before the door. At the entrance, a giant image of Ronald McDonald waves visitors in, a fitting greeter for a McDonald's built to be bigger than all the rest.

## Flight Context

The World's Largest Entertainment McDonald's is at approximately 28.4505°N, 81.4715°W on Sand Lake Road in Orlando's tourist district, a short distance south of the Universal Orlando Resort. From 1,500–2,500 feet, a single restaurant is hard to pick out, so use the Universal parks to the north as a reference. Nearby airports: Orlando International Airport (KMCO) about 10 statute miles east, inside Orlando's Class B airspace; Kissimmee Gateway Airport (KISM) about 11 statute miles south-southeast; and Orlando Executive Airport (KORL) about 11 statute miles northeast. Summer afternoon thunderstorms are common over central Florida.
