# World's Largest Office Chair

A 33-foot steel office chair built in 1981 to sell furniture became such a landmark that Anniston's chamber of commerce reportedly flew visiting businessmen over it.

**Location:** 33.6526, -85.8296

**Categories:** 1981 establishments in Alabama, Anniston, Alabama, Tourist attractions in Calhoun County, Alabama

## Introduction

Anniston's chamber of commerce reportedly used to fly visiting businessmen over a chair. Not just any chair. Rising 33 feet above the lot next to a furniture showroom, the World's Largest Office Chair became a major landmark for the city after it was finished in 1981 - which was exactly the kind of attention its builder had been hoping for, even if he had a narrower audience in mind.

## Inches Into Feet

Leonard "Sonny" Miller, owner of Miller's Office Supply, wanted something that would draw attention to his furniture business. His solution was simple arithmetic. He took an office chair from The HON Company as the model and converted its dimensions from inches to feet. Construction began in 1981 on the empty lot next to the showroom, using 10 tons of steel and 15 tons of cement, and the finished chair is said to be built to withstand 85 mph winds. It stands 33 feet tall and 19 feet wide, and its seat is a 15-foot square. To reach that seat, the builders added a spiral staircase, letting visitors climb up and sit, in a manner of speaking, in a chair built for a giant. The work was completed in May 1981.

## The Fine Print on a Big Title

Big roadside attractions tend to come with big claims, and this one is no exception. Despite claims that the chair was officially recognized as the world's largest office chair, Guinness World Records never recognized that record. The sign on the chair itself is more modest than the legend: it lists only an official recognition, in 1982, as the world's largest chair - without the word "office." Even that title did not last. By one account, in 1995 it passed to a seven-story chair in Manzano, Italy. Anniston's chair, though, has held on to its unofficial distinction as the world's largest office chair - a title no one official ever granted, but one the chair has kept all the same.

## Rust and Return

Landmarks need upkeep, and for a while this one did not get much. During the 1990s, Miller's moved across town, and the lot holding the chair was put up for sale. The spiral staircase to the seat was removed. After years of little maintenance, the giant chair grew weathered and rusty. Then the story turned. Years later, Miller's moved back to its original location, and the chair was repainted. It even had a turn on national television, appearing in the fourth episode of The Amazing Race: Family Edition. More than four decades after Sonny Miller converted inches into feet, his advertising stunt is still standing on Noble Street in Anniston, a roadside landmark for the business it was built to promote.

## Flight Context

The chair stands in Anniston, Alabama, at approximately 33.653°N, 85.830°W - fittingly for a landmark once shown off from the air. Anniston Regional Airport (KANB) is only about 4 nautical miles to the south-southwest, so a local flight from KANB makes an easy trip.
