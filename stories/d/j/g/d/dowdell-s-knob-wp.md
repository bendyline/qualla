# Dowdell's Knob

The highest point in Harris County, Georgia, is the overlook where Franklin D. Roosevelt picnicked at a stone grill above the Pine Mountain Range - and where a statue of him now greets visitors.

**Location:** 32.8407, -84.7458

**Categories:** Mountains of Georgia (U.S. state), Landforms of Harris County, Georgia

## Introduction

A President of the United States left his stone grill on top of a Georgia mountain. Franklin D. Roosevelt, visiting nearby Warm Springs, liked to dine occasionally on Dowdell's Knob, and the grill he used for picnics there is still in place. The spot is still remembered for those meals - and for the view that came with them, from the highest point in Harris County, 1,395 feet above sea level on the crest of the Pine Mountain Range.

## The President's Picnic Ground

In 1924, Roosevelt came to this part of Georgia, drawn by naturally warm springs that offered relief from polio. His visits to Warm Springs brought him within reach of Dowdell's Knob, and the mountaintop became one of his retreats. Georgia's state parks describe it simply as the place where the president "sometimes picnicked and pondered world affairs." That phrase carries weight when you consider who was doing the pondering. The stone grill and picnic area he used turned a quiet summit into a dining room with a horizon for walls, one he shared with polio patients and dignitaries alike. Today a life-size sculpture of Roosevelt welcomes visitors to the overlook, and historical markers stand nearby to explain why a president's likeness waits on a Georgia mountaintop.

## Highest on the Ridge

Dowdell's Knob is a prominent mountain in its own right. At 1,395 feet, it is the highest point in Harris County and the highest point on the Pine Mountain Range in the area, with a topographic prominence of about 495 feet. It sits near the community of Shiloh, about 66 miles south of Atlanta and 30 miles north of Columbus, and Georgia State Route 190 offers the easiest route to the top. The overlook stands above King's Gap, looking out across the landscape that Roosevelt came to know over his years of visits. The summit takes its name not from the president but from James and Lewis Dowdell, early settlers of the area.

## A Park Built Around a View

The knob lies within F.D. Roosevelt State Park, which at 10,874 acres is Georgia's largest state park. The park bills itself, fairly enough, as "a hiker's and backpacker's haven." More than 40 miles of trails wind through the park, including the popular 23-mile Pine Mountain Trail. Several of the park's amenities were built by the Civilian Conservation Corps during the Great Depression, including cottages and the Liberty Bell swimming pool. For visitors who make the drive up to Dowdell's Knob, the reward is the same one Roosevelt enjoyed: a seat at the top of the ridge and the long view beyond.

## Flight Context

Dowdell's Knob stands on the Pine Mountain Range in Harris County, Georgia, at approximately 32.841°N, 84.746°W, with a summit elevation of 1,395 feet MSL. Fly at least 3,000-3,500 feet MSL and follow the ridge line of the Pine Mountain Range to find the overlook. Harris County Airport (KPIM) at Pine Mountain is about 7 nautical miles to the west, and Roosevelt Memorial Airport (5A9) near Warm Springs is about 6 nautical miles to the north-northeast. Columbus Airport (KCSG) is about 22 nautical miles south-southwest, and Hartsfield-Jackson Atlanta International (KATL) about 50 nautical miles north-northeast.
