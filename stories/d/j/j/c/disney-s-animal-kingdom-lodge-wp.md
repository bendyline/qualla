# Disney's Animal Kingdom Lodge

At this African-themed Walt Disney World resort, giraffes, zebras and flamingos graze on savannas just beyond the balconies, and even the restaurant names speak Swahili.

**Location:** 28.3536, -81.6028

**Categories:** Hotels in Walt Disney World Resort, Hotels established in 2001, Disney's Animal Kingdom, Wildlife viewing, African-themed architecture

## Introduction

Boma is a word in Swahili and several other African languages for an enclosure, a fort or a pen for livestock. Jiko means "a cooking place." Maji means water. At Disney's Animal Kingdom Lodge, the vocabulary is part of the scenery, and so are the animals: look out from a guest room or a lobby window and the view may be of wildlife grazing on a savanna. Since April 16, 2001, this African-themed deluxe resort at Walt Disney World has tried to make central Florida feel like somewhere else entirely.

## A Savanna at the Door

The lodge sits in the Animal Kingdom Resort Area, next to Disney's Animal Kingdom theme park, and its central idea is proximity. Guests can watch wildlife from their rooms and from the resort's public spaces, close enough to feel as though they are staying on an African savanna. The roll call of residents is long. Reticulated giraffes, Grant's zebras, greater kudu, impala, okapi, eland, gemsbok, waterbuck, bongo, red river hogs and white-bearded wildebeest share the grounds with Ankole cattle and Thomson's gazelles. Birds range from ostriches, Marabou storks and Rüppell's griffon vultures to blue cranes, East African crowned cranes, pink-backed pelicans and greater flamingos, while radiated tortoises move along at their own pace.

## Built by a Lodge Maker

The hotel was designed by Denver architect Peter Dominick of Urban Design Group, the firm now known as 4240 Architecture. Grand lodges were something of a specialty for him: he also designed Disney's Wilderness Lodge and Disney's Grand Californian Hotel & Spa. The resort soon grew. On October 11, 2006, Disney announced that the lodge would become home to a new Disney Vacation Club property, Disney's Animal Kingdom Villas, and the first villas in the original building opened in 2007, though one account dates the first phase's completion to February 2008. A separate building, Kidani Village, rose to the west of the original lodge with more than 300 villas. The Sunset Savanna was extended to reach it, and more species arrived. Kidani Village opened 65 percent of its rooms on May 1, 2009, at which point the original building was renamed Jambo House, and the rest were completed that September.

## Around the Wood Fires

Food carries the theme as strongly as the animals do. Jiko, The Cooking Place, is the resort's Signature Dining restaurant, serving African-style cooking with influences from the Mediterranean coast, India and Europe alongside South African wines. Its earth-toned dining room, designed by Jeffrey Beers International to evoke an African lodge, wraps around an open kitchen with twin wood-burning stoves, and a private Cape Town Wine Room seats up to forty among wine-based sculptures. Boma, Flavors of Africa, is laid out like an open marketplace, with cooks working in view of diners and a buffet drawing on dishes from fifty African countries. Perched above it, the Victoria Falls Lounge pours African wines, beers and cocktails alongside a small menu of African-inspired bar fare. Over in Kidani Village, Sanaa overlooks the savanna and takes its cue from the historic spice route linking Africa, India, China and Europe, turning out dishes from tandoor ovens and slow-cooked specialties.

## Water, Play and Swahili Names

Two main pools anchor the resort: Uzima Springs (which Disney's own page now calls the Uzima Pool) at Jambo House and Samawati Springs at Kidani Village, both with zero-depth entries and waterslides set in lush African-inspired surroundings. Samawati Springs adds Uwanja Camp, a water playground with water cannons, play structures and a tipping bucket, and its pool bar is called Maji, the Swahili word for water. Children can burn off energy at the Hakuna Matata Playground, which overlooks the flamingo area and the savanna beyond. For quieter recovery there is Zahanati Massage & Fitness Center at Jambo House, open around the clock to resort guests with a steam room and sauna; Disney now directs spa and salon services to the Grand Floridian Spa. After a day in the parks, even a swim here comes with a view of the wild.

## Flight Context

Disney's Animal Kingdom Lodge is near 28.3536 N, 81.6028 W, in the Animal Kingdom Resort Area of Walt Disney World, next to Disney's Animal Kingdom theme park and about 20 miles southwest of Orlando. From outside the property, look for lodge buildings set among open savanna grassland, with Kidani Village standing west of the original Jambo House. Walt Disney World lies under a permanent FAA flight restriction (a 3-nautical-mile radius from the surface up to 3,000 feet AGL, centered near the Magic Kingdom about 4 nm north-northeast of the lodge, with limited exceptions), so keep well clear, plan any sightseeing from outside the restricted area and check current NOTAMs. Kissimmee Gateway Airport (KISM) is about 10 nm to the east-southeast and Orlando International (KMCO) about 16 nm to the east-northeast. Central Florida summer afternoons often bring fast-building thunderstorms.
