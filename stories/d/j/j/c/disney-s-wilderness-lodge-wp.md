# Disney's Wilderness Lodge

Modeled on Yellowstone's Old Faithful Inn, this Bay Lake resort brings lodgepole pine, totem poles, a Grand Canyon fireplace and even its own geyser to the edge of the Magic Kingdom.

**Location:** 28.4094, -81.5726

**Categories:** Hotels in Walt Disney World Resort, Hotels established in 1994, Disney Vacation Club, Rustic architecture, Bay Lake, Florida

## Introduction

Central Florida has a geyser. It is artificial, of course, set among man-made hot springs on the grounds of a hotel modeled on the Old Faithful Inn in Yellowstone National Park, and it sits on the shore of Bay Lake, a short boat ride from the Magic Kingdom. Disney's Wilderness Lodge, opened on May 28, 1994, is an exercise in transplanting the great lodges of the American West, timber, stone and all, to a subtropical lakeshore.

## The West, Relocated

The lodge stands in the Magic Kingdom Resort Area on Bay Lake, near Disney's Fort Wilderness Resort & Campground. It was designed by Peter Dominick, who drew on the atmosphere of the national parks of the western United States and blended natural materials with Native American elements. The main building takes its cue from the Old Faithful Inn, and the resort grounds add an artificial geyser and hot springs to complete the illusion. A similarly themed resort, Disney's Grand Californian Hotel & Spa, stands at the Disneyland Resort in California, and Dominick designed that one too. The Wilderness Lodge is rated a four-star deluxe resort, and in November 2007 it was designated part of the Florida Green Lodging Program.

## Pine, Stone and Story

The resort is built around a soaring lobby of lodgepole pine imported from Oregon and Montana. It has 55-foot totem poles, and an 82-foot fireplace is built to represent the colorful rock layers of the Grand Canyon. The display draws on the artifacts, myths, legends and stories of Northwestern Native American peoples, so the architecture doubles as a kind of gallery. Even the suites carry the theme; one is named for Yosemite. Guests have restaurants, a 24-hour fitness center (Disney currently directs spa and salon services to the Grand Floridian Spa), themed pools, hot tubs, a kids' zone and babysitting center, and fun and educational activities for adults and children.

## Skillets, Stick Horses and Campfires

Dining leans into the frontier theme. Whispering Canyon Cafe is an Old West eatery featuring all-you-can-eat skillets and wooden stick horses. Storybook Dining at Artist Point pairs classic American food with a meet-and-greet featuring Snow White and the Seven Dwarfs. Geyser Point Bar & Grill sits on the waterfront, Roaring Fork serves quick meals from breakfast to dinner, and the Territory Lounge pours craft brews and wine. Copper Creek Springs, the main pool, has a 67-foot waterslide, and Boulder Ridge Cove is the second. Evenings bring campfire activities and movies under the stars, and by day guests can join guided fishing excursions or rent bikes and motorboats. Boats carry guests across the water to the Magic Kingdom (service was temporarily suspended at times during the lodge's 2026 exterior refurbishment), while buses reach all four theme parks, both water parks and Disney Springs.

## Railroad Men and Lakeside Cabins

Two Disney Vacation Club properties have joined the lodge. The first, announced in 1998 as The Villas at Disney's Wilderness Lodge, opened on November 15, 2000, and is now called the Boulder Ridge Villas. Where the main lodge evokes the national park hotels of the West, the villas are themed after the lodgings built by railroad workers in the late 19th century. Railroad artwork and memorabilia fill the villas' main building, including two cars from Walt Disney's own rideable miniature railroad, the Carolwood Pacific, displayed in a room off the lobby. Accommodations range from studios with kitchenettes to one- and two-bedroom villas with fuller kitchens and living space. In late 2015 Disney began another extensive building project at the resort, and on July 17, 2017, the Copper Creek Villas & Cabins opened. They include 26 Cascade Cabins along the shoreline, while the entire south wing of the original lodge was converted from hotel rooms into 158 Vacation Club units.

## Flight Context

Disney's Wilderness Lodge is near 28.4094 N, 81.5726 W on the shore of Bay Lake, in the Magic Kingdom Resort Area of Walt Disney World about 20 miles southwest of Orlando, near Fort Wilderness and across the water from the Contemporary Resort's A-frame. Bay Lake, about a mile across, is the obvious landmark. Walt Disney World lies under a permanent FAA flight restriction (a 3-nautical-mile radius from the surface up to 3,000 feet AGL, centered very close to this site, with limited exceptions), so view the area from outside the restricted zone and check current NOTAMs. Kissimmee Gateway Airport (KISM) is about 10 nm to the southeast, Orlando International (KMCO) about 14 nm to the east, and Orlando Executive (KORL) about 15 nm to the east-northeast. Summer afternoon thunderstorms are common.
