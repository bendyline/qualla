# Billy Graham Library

A museum built to look like a dairy barn, a few miles from the Charlotte farm where Billy Graham grew up, tells the evangelist's story and holds the prayer garden where he and his wife Ruth are buried.

**Location:** 35.1906, -80.9093

**Categories:** Billy Graham, Biographical museums in North Carolina, Libraries in North Carolina, Museums in Charlotte, North Carolina, Religious museums in North Carolina

## Introduction

On March 2, 2018, mourners for Billy Graham gathered under a tent. The tent recalled the ones in which the evangelist had held his first crusades, and now, nine days after his death on February 21, his funeral was held in one on the grounds of the library that bears his name in Charlotte, North Carolina. Before the service, Graham had lain in repose in his childhood homeplace next to the library and then lain in honor at the United States Capitol in Washington. Afterward, he was buried beside his wife in the library's prayer garden, a few miles from the farm where he grew up.

## A Barn for a Farm Boy

The Billy Graham Library is a public museum and library documenting Graham's life and ministry. The 40,000-square-foot complex, which opened to the public on June 5, 2007, is styled after a dairy barn, a nod to Graham's upbringing on a dairy farm on the outskirts of Charlotte. There is even a mechanical "talking" cow. Visitors enter through a doorway shaped like a cross, and the main exhibit, called the Journey of Faith, follows Graham's path from his beginnings as a young farm boy in rural North Carolina. The library stands on the grounds of the international headquarters of the Billy Graham Evangelistic Association.

## Home Place and Dairy Bar

The farm theme runs throughout. Graham's boyhood home, the house of his mother, Morrow Coffey Graham, has been moved to the library grounds and restored a short walk from the main library building. Inside the library, a model Graham Brothers Dairy truck is on display, and a replica of Graham's desk gives a glimpse of his working life. A gallery is devoted to Ruth Bell Graham, his wife. Visitors can stop at the Graham Brothers Dairy Bar for something to eat and browse the shelves of Ruth's Attic bookstore. For a museum about a famous preacher, the place keeps returning to a single idea: that his story started on a farm on the edge of Charlotte.

## The Prayer Garden

The library had been open only twelve days when it became a burial ground. On June 13, 2007, as Ruth Bell Graham lay gravely ill, her husband announced that they had decided to be buried beside each other at the library. She died the next day, and was buried in the Memorial Prayer Garden on June 17. Others from Graham's long ministry followed. In March 2009, Wilma "Billie" Barrows, wife of Graham's longtime music director Cliff Barrows, was interred on the library grounds. In April 2013 the gospel singer George Beverly Shea, Graham's regular featured soloist, was laid to rest there, and Cliff Barrows was buried beside his wife after his death in November 2016. In 2018 Billy Graham joined them. An Appreciation Walkway now leads visitors to the graves of Billy and Ruth Graham.

## Flight Context

The Billy Graham Library is at 35.1906°N, 80.9093°W in southwest Charlotte, North Carolina, only about 2 nm southeast of Charlotte Douglas International Airport (KCLT). The barn-style library sits on the campus of the Billy Graham Evangelistic Association headquarters. Because the site lies so close to a major airline hub, any sightseeing overflight must be planned around Charlotte's arrival and departure traffic; check the sectional and work with Charlotte's air traffic controllers. If cleared, about 2,500-3,000 feet MSL gives a good view. Alternatives for general aviation include Gastonia Municipal Airport (KAKH), about 12 nm west, and Concord-Padgett Regional Airport (KJQF), about 15 nm northeast. Summer haze and afternoon thunderstorms are common.
