# Mast General Store

A nickel has long bought a cup of coffee on the honor system at the Valle Crucis general store that has sold everything a mountain community needed since 1883 - and still handles its mail.

**Location:** 36.2078, -81.7800

**Categories:** General stores in the United States, Commercial buildings on the National Register of Historic Places in North Carolina, Tourist attractions in Watauga County, North Carolina, Commercial buildings completed in 1883, Blue Ridge Mountains

## Introduction

Coffee has long cost a nickel at the Mast General Store, and the arrangement runs on trust: you pay on the honor system. That small custom has outlasted a great deal in Valle Crucis, a community in North Carolina's Watauga County, where this general store still does the job general stores were built to do. It houses the local post office, ZIP code 28691, and remains the center of community life. The National Register of Historic Places recognizes it as one of the best remaining examples of its type. For more than 140 years, this is where the valley has come for its mail and its supplies.

## Taylor, Then Mast

Henry Taylor built the store in 1882, and it opened for business the following year. Around 1897, W.W. Mast bought a half interest, and the name changed to mark the partnership: the Taylor and Mast General Store. By 1913 Mast had bought out the rest of Taylor's share, and the business took the name it carries today. The Mast family kept it for three generations. Their slogan was part boast and part dare - "if you can't buy it here, you don't need it" - a nod to the enormous variety of goods for sale. The family era ended in 1973, when the store was sold to a doctor from Atlanta and a professor from Appalachian State University. That same year, on April 3, 1973, the Valle Crucis store was added to the National Register of Historic Places.

## Dark Doors and a Second Life

Even a landmark can falter. The business closed its doors in 1977, and they stayed shut until John and Faye Cooper, who had bought the store in 1979, reopened it in June 1980. What followed was a slow, steady expansion. In 1988 the first Mast General Store outside the Valle Crucis area opened in Boone. Waynesville followed in 1991, Hendersonville in August 1995 and Asheville in 1999. In the mid-1990s, between those openings, the company became employee-owned; it dates the change to 1995. The store crossed its first state line on March 12, 2003, opening in Greenville, South Carolina, and reached Gay Street in downtown Knoxville, Tennessee, in August 2006. Columbia, South Carolina, got the ninth store on May 25, 2011, Winston-Salem the tenth in May 2015, and Roanoke, Virginia, the eleventh in 2020. Not every idea stuck: a smaller concept shop, Mast Store Candy and More, opened in Blowing Rock in March 2005 and has since closed.

## Hundreds of Kinds of Candy

The old slogan still feels half-serious when you look at what the shelves hold. There are old-time hearth and home goods and outdoor clothing and gear. Footwear and work clothes sit alongside wind-up toys of the old-fashioned kind, regional books and music, and honey. Then there is the candy - hundreds of varieties of old-fashioned sweets, enough to turn choosing a single piece into a serious decision. Of the eleven locations, two stand in Valle Crucis itself: the original store and its annex. Out-of-town visitors browse the merchandise, but the mail still needs sorting, and the coffee still needs paying for. The store has never fully turned into a museum of itself. It still serves the people who live around it.

## Minding the Old and the New

Preservation North Carolina gave the store its L. Vincent Lowe, Jr. Business Award in 1998. The award goes to businesses that make special efforts to assist or promote historic preservation in the state, and the store's own building stands within the Valle Crucis Historic District. In 2006 the company looked forward as well as back. It pledged to buy renewable "Green Energy" as part of a campaign by NCGreenPower to expand the use of renewable power in North Carolina. It is a fitting mix for a store that sells wind-up toys and outdoor gear side by side: one foot planted firmly in 1883, the other stepping carefully into whatever comes next.

## Flight Context

The Mast General Store sits at approximately 36.2078 N, 81.7800 W in the small community of Valle Crucis, in the Blue Ridge Mountains of Watauga County, North Carolina, a few miles west of Boone. The terrain around the valley is high and rugged, so plan a viewing altitude well above the surrounding ridges (around 7,000-8,000 ft MSL) and descend only with good visibility. Nearby airports: Johnson County Airport, Mountain City, Tennessee (FAA identifier 6A4, no ICAO code; about 13 nm north); Avery County Airport/Morrison Field, Spruce Pine (FAA identifier 7A8, no ICAO code; about 19 nm south-southwest); Hickory Regional Airport (KHKY; about 34 nm southeast); and Tri-Cities Airport, Blountville, Tennessee (KTRI; about 34 nm west-northwest, with scheduled airline service).
