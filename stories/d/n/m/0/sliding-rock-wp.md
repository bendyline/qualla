# Sliding Rock

In the Pisgah National Forest near Brevard, North Carolina, a 60-foot sheet of rock on Looking Glass Creek serves as a natural water slide into a deep, 55-degree pool.

**Location:** 35.3110, -82.7873

**Categories:** Waterfalls of North Carolina, Pisgah National Forest, Protected areas of Transylvania County, North Carolina, Swimming holes

## Introduction

The first thing everyone remembers is the cold. The water pouring over Sliding Rock runs around 55 degrees Fahrenheit even in summer, and on a hot summer afternoon that shock is exactly the point. Sit down at the top, let Looking Glass Creek take you, and about 60 feet of gently sloping stone deliver you into a deep plunge pool with a strong, churning current. Adventurous tourists and locals have long treated this small waterfall in the Pisgah National Forest near Brevard, North Carolina, as something rarer than a view: a waterfall you are allowed to ride.

## Sixty Feet of Stone

Sliding Rock is a slide-type waterfall, a long, gently sloping face of rock that Looking Glass Creek slides down rather than plunges over. It ends in a large pool roughly eight feet deep, up to ten by the Forest Service's account, and the current there is cold and fast enough that anyone going in needs to be able to swim.

The U.S. Forest Service has developed the site into a popular recreation area, staffed by a private recreation company, Naventure according to the Forest Service's current Sliding Rock page, which also manages other recreation areas and campgrounds. There are steps down to the pool, a railing on the left side of the rock for the climb back up, and a viewing platform for anyone who would rather watch. There used to be two platforms, but the lower one was reportedly destroyed when a rainstorm flooded the creek in the summer of 2021.

## Rules of the Ride

There is one approved way down: sitting upright. Sliders must be able to swim, and the Forest Service is blunt about it: if you cannot swim, you cannot slide, no exceptions. The only flotation allowed is an approved personal flotation device; floats, tubes, noodles, inflatables and boards are not. Children below a certain age slide with the help of a capable adult.

Lifeguards are on duty from 9 a.m. to 6 p.m. every day from Memorial Day through Labor Day, when the restrooms are open too, and a per-person fee is charged (the Forest Service lists $6, with children five and under free). When the site is not staffed, there are no lifeguards and the restrooms are closed. Whatever the season, the rock may close to sliders during high water, heavy rain or lightning.

## Up the Road Past the Falls

Getting there is part of the pleasure. From the intersection of US 276, US 64 and NC 280 in Pisgah Forest, head north on US 276 for about 7.7 miles. Along the way you pass Looking Glass Falls and the parking area for Moore Cove Falls, two of the many waterfalls in this corner of the forest; others nearby include Slick Rock Falls, Cedar Rock Falls, Cove Creek Falls, Daniel Ridge Falls and Twin Falls.

Parking is in a lot beside the highway above the rock. On busy summer days it fills, and the lot is closed until spaces open again. Down at the creek, meanwhile, the sliders keep coming, one after another, into the cold.

## Flight Context

Sliding Rock is at 35.3110°N, 82.7873°W on Looking Glass Creek in the Pisgah National Forest, Transylvania County, North Carolina, beside US 276 about 7.7 road miles north of Pisgah Forest near Brevard. The rock itself is too small to pick out from altitude; follow the US 276 corridor up the forested valley, with Looking Glass Falls a short way south along the road. This is steep, heavily wooded Blue Ridge terrain with ridges well above the valley floor, so plan at least 2,000 ft above the surrounding ridgelines. Nearby airports: Asheville Regional Airport (KAVL), about 14 nm east-northeast, with Class C airspace, and Hendersonville Airport (FAA identifier 0A7, no ICAO code), about 17 nm east. Mountain weather shifts quickly; summer afternoons bring thunderstorms, which also close the slide.
